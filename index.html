Collaborative AR for iPhone, iPad & Apple Vision Pro

See the same model, in the same place.

Place detailed 3D anatomy in your room and explore it with others on a FaceTime call. Everyone sees it where you put it, and every change shows up for everyone at once.

How it works

Free · No account · No ads or tracking

Start a FaceTime call. Put a model on the table.

  1. Start a session

    Choose Local Session to explore on your own, or SharePlay Session to bring in everyone on your FaceTime call.

  2. Pick a model

    Nine detailed anatomy models are built in, from a single neuron to the whole digestive tract. You can also import your own USDZ files.

  3. Place it in the room

    Tap a table or the floor. Move, turn and resize it with your fingers.

  4. Walk around it together

    Lean in to see the layers. Whatever anyone moves, pins or notes appears for everyone, and people who join late see the scene as it is.

Apple Vision Pro

On Vision Pro, the specimen stands in the room with you.

Reach out and move it with your hands, set exact position and scale in the control panel, or have its description read aloud. People on iPhone and iPad can join the same session.

What you can do in a session

Bring your own models
Import any USDZ file from Files. It is kept in your iCloud, so it is on your iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro, and anyone in your session can load it.
Pins and notes
Drop a coloured pin on the exact structure you mean and add a note. Everyone sees it in the same spot.
Share on your terms
Choose who can view or edit each model, whether they can pass it on, and stop sharing whenever you like. Links made for a session end with it.
iPhone and Vision Pro, lined up
Link an iPhone with an Apple Vision Pro: the phone shows a marker, the headset reads it, and both see the model in the same place.

Used in anatomy classes, by study groups on FaceTime, and for design reviews with your own USDZ files.
