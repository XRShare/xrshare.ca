02 — How it works

From a FaceTime call to a shared specimen table in under a minute.

  1. 01

    Start a session

    Open a Local Session to practise alone, or start a SharePlay Session from a FaceTime call to bring others in.

  2. 02

    Pick a model

    Choose from the built-in anatomy library (skin, heart, neuron, cell, digestive system and more) or import your own USDZ files.

  3. 03

    Place it in the room

    Tap a surface to place it. Move, rotate and scale with familiar gestures, then walk up close to study the details.

  4. 04

    Explore together

    Everyone in the session sees the same models in the same place. Every move is synced live, and people who join late catch up automatically.

03 — On iPhone & iPad

Your phone becomes a window into the shared scene.

04 — On Apple Vision Pro

Models at full scale, all around the room.

On Apple Vision Pro, models float in your space and respond to your hands. Use the control panel for exact placement, read or listen to each model's description, and join the same SharePlay session as people on iPhone and iPad.

05 — Features

More than looking. Working together.

Everything in XRShare is built to be shared. What one person does, everyone in the session sees.

Bring your own

Your models, in the room

Import any USDZ file from Files. It is saved to your iCloud, so it appears on your iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro. Add it to a session and everyone can load it.

Pins & notes

Point at exactly what you mean

Drop coloured pins on a model and attach notes to them. Everyone in the session sees them instantly, in the same spot.

Model Library

Share on your terms

Share models with iCloud links, choose whether each person can view or edit, decide whether they can share onward, and stop sharing at any time.

iPhone + Vision Pro

One room, every device

Link an iPhone with an Apple Vision Pro: the phone shows a marker on its screen, and the headset uses it to line up both views of the room.

Participants

Know who’s there

See everyone in the session by name, who is hosting, and how many models are in the scene.

Vision Pro

Precise and hands-free

Grab models with your hands, set exact position, rotation and scale in the control panel, and have each model’s description read aloud with Narrate.

Sessions
Local Session for working alone offline. SharePlay Session over FaceTime for everyone else.
Library
Nine detailed anatomy models with descriptions, plus favourites and your own imports.
Devices
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 26 or later.
Price
Free, with no account to create, no ads and no tracking.

Privacy

We don’t collect your data.

  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No XRShare servers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • Camera images are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Session data goes only to the people in your SharePlay session. Models you import are stored in your own iCloud account.
